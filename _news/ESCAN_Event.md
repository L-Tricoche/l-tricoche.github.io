It was a great pleasure to attend the [ESCAN](https://bsky.app/profile/escaneu.bsky.social) conference in Rome this June. The meeting offered rich and stimulating scientific exchanges, and was a wonderful opportunity to connect with the social and affective neuroscience community.

I had the chance to take part in the pre-conference workshop, during which we discussed the future major challenges facing social neuroscience, a thought-provoking session that gave rise to many inspiring conversations.

I also had the opportunity to present results from our new study on ecological behaviours, currently under revision. The preprint is available [here](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/9kspn_v1).
